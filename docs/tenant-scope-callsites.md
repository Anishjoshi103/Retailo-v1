## Identity-model classification

- `TenantOwnedModel` subclasses (for example `Branch`, `Product`, `Supplier`, `InventoryBalance`, `PurchaseReceipt`, `PurchaseInvoice`, and their tenant-owned lines) require tenant scope for normal application queries.
- `Tenant`, `User`, and `Membership` are identity/authorization models, not ordinary tenant-owned business data. Queries still need explicit constraints appropriate to the auth flow; do not blindly add `.for_tenant()` where the model does not inherit `TenantOwnedModel`.
- `BranchAccess` is a membership-to-branch grant model, not a `TenantOwnedModel`; validate both membership and branch ownership/access in its use sites.
